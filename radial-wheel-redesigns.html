Action wheel: Halo and three variants

The current wheel is shown first for comparison. Halo is the one you picked. Each variant keeps its frosted ring and changes one part: the selection marker, the inner ring, or the center. All of them keep the same behaviour: hold Alt+Q, point to choose (the pointer angle counts even outside the ring), use the arrow keys, click a group to enter it, right-click to go back, and use the center to edit the config. Only the look and the motion change.
