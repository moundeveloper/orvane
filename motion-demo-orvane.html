Orvane motion pass: six components

Copies of real Orvane components, made to look and behave like the app. Switch between Current (how they move today) and Smoothed (the proposal). The proposal uses one gentle curve, cubic-bezier(.3,.1,.2,1). It starts softly (about 5% of the motion in the first frame), so nothing jumps, then slows down to rest. Everything fades in from 0. It follows Orvane's motion rules: no bounce, no stagger, closing is instant, and nearby content never animates.

M toggles

Date picker

pickers/DatePicker.vue + MonthYearGrid.vue. Change months, then click the month name.

Now: the popover reveals, but months and the month grid swap instantly.Smoothed: the popover fades in from 0 and moves 4px, 220 ms. Days slide 10px from the side you're moving toward and fade in from 0, 260 ms. The month grid scales from .97, and back to days from 1.03, 240 ms. The title fades in, 200 ms.

Calendar month

CalendarView.vue. Click days, and use ‹ › and Today.

Now: the selection ring jumps and months swap instantly.Smoothed: the selection ring glides to the clicked day, 240 ms, transform only. Months slide 10px and fade in from 0, 260 ms. The ring rides along with the month.

Folder tree

FileFolderTree.vue (File Cabinet). Expand and collapse folders.

Now: the chevron icon swaps and children pop in.Smoothed: the chevron rotates 90°, 200 ms. Children fade in from 0 and move from −4px as one block, 220 ms. Collapsing is instant. The rows below move instantly, never animated.

Switches

BrowserDrawer.vue settings rows. Click a row or a switch.

Now: the knob animates left (a layout property), 150 ms ease. The track colour snaps.Smoothed: the knob moves on transform, 220 ms. The track colour fades on the same curve and timing.

Number field

CustomNumber.vue. Click or hold − / +, or use ↑ ↓ in the field.

Now: the value swaps instantly.Smoothed: the old value fades out as the new one fades in on the same spot, 180 ms, with no movement. The number never goes blank. Held steps are instant.

Tabs

SplitTabs.vue. Switch tabs, add with +, close with ×.

Now: new tabs and pane switches are instant.Smoothed: a new tab fades in from 0 and 3px below, 220 ms. Switching fades the pane in from 0, 180 ms, with no movement, so terminals don't shift. Closing stays instant.
