Curve lab

The same "window opens" motion with four curves. Each graph shows progress over time. The dots on the first graph are the window widths measured frame by frame in the video (00:19.95).

What makes it feel smooth: the window curve finishes most of its travel in the first 100 ms, then settles slowly with no bounce. Short UI uses OutCubic. Panels close 2.2× faster than they open. Opacity and transform have their own timing. Only transform, opacity and clip-path animate.

Approximations: the carousel scroll uses a critically damped spring in place of Qt's SmoothedAnimation. The panel tilt uses real CSS perspective; Qt's plain Rotation has none. The tiling is dwindle-like: each new window splits the last one.
